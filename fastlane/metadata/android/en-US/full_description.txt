A lightweight, modern Android audio player designed for seamless local playback and online streaming with a fluid Material 3 interface.

Features:

* Ad-free streaming with full background playback
* Seamless account sync for personal playlists, liked tracks, and subscriptions
* Universal local audio file support (FLAC, MP3, AAC, OPUS, WAV)
* Lightweight engine optimized for instant startup and low battery consumption
* Private, ad-free, and tracker-free listening environment
* EBU R128 auto-loudness normalization
* Variable tempo, pitch, and speed adjustment tools
* Gapless playback and smooth track crossfading
* System-level equalizer and spatial audio compatibility
* Synchronized, real-time live lyrics
* Instant lyric translation and romanization
* Ambient music identification to detect songs playing nearby
* Detailed listening history and audio statistics
* Discord Rich Presence status sharing
* Modern Material You design system
* Dynamic palette extraction from current album art
* Adaptive layouts tailored for standard screens, foldables, and tablets
* Deep interface, gesture, and playback customization
* Configurable transitions, animations, and home view modularity
